About the workshop

Useful things, thoughtfully explained.

Bill's Workshop Finds is the recommendation side of Bill's Workshop Company—a place for practical guides instead of endless product grids.

Hi, I'm Bill.

I'm a web developer, builder, problem-solver, licensed private pilot and the person behind Bill's Workshop Company. I have always appreciated products that earn their space by making a real task easier.

This site brings that approach to travel gear, gifts, dog-lover finds, teacher resources and everyday problem-solving. Each guide explains the need first, then the features worth comparing. The goal is to help you make a confident choice—even if the best choice is not the most expensive one.

How recommendations are selected

Items and product categories are considered for usefulness, suitability for the stated purpose, clarity of features and overall value. Inclusion is not guaranteed by an affiliate relationship, and companies cannot purchase a favorable opinion.

Part of Bill's Workshop Company

The main workshop creates practical digital tools. The Etsy shop is home to original profession-and-dog apparel and gifts. Bill's Workshop Finds connects those worlds through useful, interest-based guides.
