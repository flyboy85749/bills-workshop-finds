15 Checkride Gifts and Prep Essentials, in the Order the Day Runs

The private pilot checkride is a written test, a three-hour oral and a flight, and most of what decides it is paperwork and recall rather than stick and rudder. This list follows that order, so you can buy for the part they are actually in.

A checkride is not one event. It is a knowledge test taken weeks earlier, a cross-country planned the night before, an oral examination across a table, and finally about ninety minutes of flying — and applicants fail the first three far more often than the last one. Almost everything useful you can buy for someone in this phase is paper.

So the list runs in that order: the knowledge test, the oral, the flight planning, the day itself, and the first hour after they pass. If they are earlier in training than this, the student pilot gift guide covers the equipment side stage by stage — its checkride prep section is where this guide picks up, and the oral exam guide and manual E6B it recommends are assumed here rather than repeated.

Two things worth knowing before you buy. Most of this is dated material—regulations, charts and question banks all expire, and an out-of-date copy actively teaches wrong answers. And if the solo is the milestone you are really marking, the first solo gift guide is the better list; this one is for the certificate.
