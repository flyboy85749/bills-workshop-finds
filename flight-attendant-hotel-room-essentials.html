15 Flight Attendant Hotel Room Essentials, Sorted by When You Reach For Them

A layover room is a workplace you sleep in. These are the finds that fix what hotel rooms reliably get wrong.

The same five problems show up in Denver, Doha and a two-star airport hotel: the door feels thin, the curtains gape, there is nowhere to make coffee, the uniform needs saving, and the room feels like nobody’s.

So this list runs in the order you reach for things: the first five minutes through the door, blacking the room out, eating without going back out, keeping the uniform wearable, and making the place feel human. Check your airline’s rules and the property’s policies before packing appliances, aerosols or anything that draws power in the room.
